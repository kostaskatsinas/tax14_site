### Office brief

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide editorial photograph for an accounting office website section. Primary request: a contemporary accounting office in Athens, Greece, calm and approachable. Scene: a tidy professional desk with a calculator, a few neatly arranged financial documents with no readable text, a closed navy folder, laptop viewed obliquely, and two comfortable visitor chairs; softly blurred shelving and large windows behind. No people. Composition: landscape 3:2, a complete scene that remains clear at 400 pixels wide, practical real office rather than a luxury showroom. Lighting: natural Mediterranean daylight, realistic soft shadows. Palette: navy blue, muted teal, light oak, white. Photographic realism and natural material textures. No logos, no lettering, no watermarks, no stock-photo decorative graphics.

Asset: `assets/accounting-office.webp`

### Handshake brief

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide editorial photograph for an accounting services website section about starting a professional collaboration. Primary request: two adult professionals, an accountant and a client, shaking hands across the corner of an office desk after a consultation. Frame their upper bodies and the single natural handshake clearly in the center; one wears a navy blazer and the other a pale blue shirt. A neat folder and calculator on the desk, a softly blurred contemporary Greek office in the background. Composition: landscape 3:2, friendly and candid, readable when shown at 400 pixels wide. Lighting: soft Mediterranean daylight. Palette: navy, muted teal details, white and light oak, matching a calm accounting office website. Natural anatomy, realistic hands with five fingers each, exactly two people and one handshake. No logos, no lettering, no watermarks, no decorative graphics.

Asset: `assets/client-handshake.webp`
