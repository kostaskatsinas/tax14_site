Frequently asked questions

About changing accountants, setting up a company and working together remotely.

Ask Petros on +30 210 202 4225

Can I change accountants during the year?

Yes, in any month. We contact your previous accountant, check myDATA and ERGANI II and transfer your records.

Sole trader or IKE?

It depends on profit and risk. Sole traders pay progressive tax of 9% to 44%; an IKE pays 22% and offers limited liability. See the calculator above.

Do you handle myDATA and the Digital Work Card?

Yes. We check income and expense classifications in myDATA before each VAT return and set up the Digital Work Card in ERGANI II.

Can we work together online?

Yes, from anywhere in Greece or abroad. Documents are sent electronically and authorisations are completed through gov.gr and Taxisnet.

What do you check before filing a tax return?

Presumptive income, the electronic receipts requirement (30%), leases, the ENFIA discount and any benefits you are entitled to, before final submission.
