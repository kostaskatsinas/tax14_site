Privacy policy

The accounting and tax office TAX14 (20 Achaion Street, 11852 Ano Petralona, Athens, email petros.thanasos@gmail.com, phone +30 210 202 4225) protects your personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU 2016/679) and Greek Law 4624/2019.

What we collect

Through the contact form, we collect only what you provide: your name or company name, phone number or email address, the service you are interested in and, if supplied, your legal structure, taxpayer category and message.

Why we use it

Only to assess your enquiry, prepare a quote and contact you. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising.

How the form is sent

After the security check, your details are sent over HTTPS to Brevo, which delivers them by email to petros.thanasos@gmail.com. To protect against automated requests, we use Cloudflare Turnstile.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or erase your data, restrict its processing and withdraw consent at any time. Write to us at petros.thanasos@gmail.com.
